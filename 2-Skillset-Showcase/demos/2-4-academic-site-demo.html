📚 My research

block: markdown · content/_index.md

My research focuses on building trustworthy, agentic and decision-oriented AI systems at the intersection of large language models, reinforcement learning and causal inference.

I study how LLM-based agents can reason, verify and self-correct when operating in high-stakes domains such as finance, healthcare and blockchain security. My work combines multi-modal learning (text, code, time-series and structured data), curriculum reinforcement learning and self-verification loops to improve robustness, interpretability and reliability.

Methodologically, I integrate empirical causal inference, large-scale benchmarking and system-level evaluation to bridge theory and deployment.

I am particularly interested in how AI systems interact with human decision-making and institutional constraints, and how these interactions reshape economic and social outcomes. I actively collaborate with researchers in academia and industry — reach out if you work on LLM agents, trustworthy AI, or AI-driven decision systems. 😃

How the site is built

Hugo Blox — pages are composed from configurable blocks in content/_index.md

The landing page stacks a resume-biography-3 block (profile, gradient mesh background, CV button) over a markdown block for the research statement, then two collection blocks — a featured article grid and a citation list — plus talks and courses. Nothing here is hand-written HTML; each section is YAML pointing at a folder of Markdown.

Configuration lives in config/_default/: hugo.yaml, params.yaml (identity, theme pack, colours), menus.yaml, languages.yaml and module.yaml. Publications can be imported from BibTeX by the import-publications GitHub Action.
