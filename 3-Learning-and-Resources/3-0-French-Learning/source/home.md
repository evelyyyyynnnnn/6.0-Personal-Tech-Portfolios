## Preview

👋 **Bonjour, tout le monde. 안녕하세요. こんにちは。**

🌐 This site is written by me, to record learning **French, Korean and
Japanese** in one place rather than three.

🌊 It started because the knowledge around me was scattered. Every time I
wanted a straight answer I spent longer searching, judging and filtering than
learning, and never built a system of my own. So I started building one.

⚠️ This site shouldn't be used for commercial purposes.

## The three languages

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| 🇫🇷 **Français** | alphabet, tenses, TEF notes | [Start →](/French/Word-Phrase/Alphabet.html) |
| 🇰🇷 **한국어** | Hangul, particles, politeness | [Start →](/Korean/Word-Phrase/Alphabet.html) |
| 🇯🇵 **日本語** | hiragana, katakana, verb groups | [Start →](/Japanese/Word-Phrase/Alphabet.html) |

Every language carries the **same four sections**, so a chapter in one has a
counterpart in the others:

- **Word & Phrase** — the writing system first, then the vocabulary that pays
- **Grammar** — the rule, examples, and where it goes wrong
- **Dialogue & Passage** — real material, not textbook sentences
- **Culture & Media** — books, film and television as listening practice

## Background

😃 This site started with me, as someone who enjoys learning languages.

💕 I began French, Japanese, Korean and Cantonese after starting university;
English and Chinese are the two I have had since childhood.

💫 Three alphabets, three grammars, one place to keep them.

## Content

📝 I share what I learn a little at a time — grammatical structure, words and
phrases, plus the media, television, books and writing of my own around them.

📖 I add emoji, video 📹, radio 📻 and notes wherever they helped me when I was
starting out.

🥹 Updating is not an easy thing. I would be very happy if this site became
your push 👊 to do one thing consistently.

☀️ Wish you a good day today.
