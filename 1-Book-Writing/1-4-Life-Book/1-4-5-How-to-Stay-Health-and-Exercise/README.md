## Features

- Daily workout schedule from May 25, 2026 onward
- Weekday routine: Face-slim AM & PM, Leg/Arm toning, Gym-run / Dance (Ballet·Zumba·Kpop) / HIT cycle
- Weekend routine: Swim + Outdoor-run or Cycle (alternating)
- Tap any pill to check off a completed workout
- Monthly progress bar and completion counter
- Navigate across 2026–2030 by year and month
